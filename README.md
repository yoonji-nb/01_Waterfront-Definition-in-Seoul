# Waterfront-Definition-in-Seoul
Definition of waterfront based on usage for five selected waterways in Seoul

This was written for my Master Thesis called "Shape of Water and Daily Life: Measuring and Leveraging the Climate-Adaptive Potentials of Seoul's Waterfronts" (https://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:7aa7a4f9-82fa-4bdc-a00e-a845fbc49763)
The objective is to defined the sunken or enclosed waterfronts in Seoul based on usage (via spatial perception), equivalent to the break of pedestrian flow inside the waterfronts. 
Five waterways were selected: Hangang river, Hongje, Jungrang, Anyang, and Tan

-Relevant research sub-question:
What is the spatial extent of a waterfront in Seoul, and the area of influence regarding heatwaves and flash floods in the summer?

-Method:
For widths, the border of adjacent motorways or main roads that run parallel to the waterways were used to define the outline of waterfronts.
For lengths, waterways were first segmented on points where they intersect with each other (which forces people to choose which route to take) and additional locations where people were forced to exit the waterfronts.
This could not be 100% automated and required many 'hands-on' processes using QGIS.

-Required inputs:
Waterways centerline and polygon datasets provided by Korean National Open Platform for Geospatial Information (www.vworld.com) -not accessible outside Korea
Automobile road centerline and polygon datasets provided by Korean National Open Platform for Geospatial Information (www.vworld.com) -not accessible outside Korea
